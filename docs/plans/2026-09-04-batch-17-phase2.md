## Scope note the spec leaves open (owner approves with this plan)

The spec's phasing assigns the knowledge read commands (`get`,
`for`, `index`, `topics`, `standing`) to no phase, while the
phase-3 reference rewrite needs every command shipped. This plan
lands them in phase 2 (T4): they share T1-T3's serde models, `for`
is one of the two call sites the dormant glob functions exist for,
and porting them here keeps phase 3 purely init/update/files plus
the rewrite.
